Architectural Transformation Breakdown

Aspect Legacy Beta Script Refactored Package Architecture
Architecture & Structure Monolithic procedural script mixing user input(), print statements, and game loop logic in single functions. Modular package (config.py, physics.py, circuit.py, cli.py) strictly separating business logic, physics, and UI wrappers.
Physics & Calculations Calculated speed but ignored horsepower or car weight in lap times; used hardcoded fuel consumption (12.5%/lap). Pure mathematical model (physics.py) dynamically computing exact lap times, fuel burn, and range based on car/engine parameters.
Testability & Randomness Untestable due to direct time.sleep() and unseeded random.random() calls inside simulation routines. Fully deterministic via injected RNG instances (rng: random.Random) and callback hooks (decide_pit, log).
Data Tracking & DNFs Failed vehicles (DNFs due to fuel/tires) were not tracked as distinct states, skewing leaderboard results. Separated active race progress from completion status; DNFs are explicitly categorized and filtered post-race.
